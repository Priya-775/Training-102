# Day 1 – Beginning My AI Training Journey

**Date:** 22 July 2026

## Today's Goal
Today was the first day of my training. The main objective was to get an overview of Generative AI and Agentic AI while understanding the fundamental concepts that will be used throughout the training.

## What I Learned

### Introduction to Agentic AI
I learned that Agentic AI is designed to perform tasks independently by planning, reasoning, and taking actions to achieve a goal. Unlike traditional AI, it can make decisions and use different tools to complete complex tasks.

### Introduction to Generative AI
I explored the basics of Generative AI and understood how it can generate new content such as text, images, code, and audio based on user prompts. It is widely used in modern AI applications.

### Large Language Models (LLMs)
I gained an introduction to Large Language Models (LLMs). I learned that models like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude are trained on massive amounts of text data, enabling them to understand context, answer questions, generate content, and assist with various tasks.

### Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
I was introduced to the concept of RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation). I understood that instead of relying only on its training data, an AI model can retrieve relevant information from external documents or databases before generating a response, making the answers more accurate and up-to-date.

### Playwright Library
I learned about the Playwright library and its role in browser automation. It can automate tasks such as opening websites, interacting with web pages, filling forms, clicking buttons, and performing testing across different browsers.

## Challenges I Faced
Most of the concepts were completely new to me. Initially, it was difficult to understand how all these technologies are connected, but as the session progressed, the overall picture became much clearer.

## Key Takeaways
The first day gave me a strong foundation in modern AI technologies. I now have a basic understanding of Agentic AI, Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, and Playwright, and I'm excited to learn how these concepts are used together in real-world AI applications.
